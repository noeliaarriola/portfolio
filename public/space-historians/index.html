THE CHALLENGE

Space history becomes meaningful when children can investigate it

Mission records, technical explanations and historical photographs contain remarkable stories. For younger audiences, however, the connections between an object, its purpose and the people behind it can be difficult to see.

Space Historians places those sources inside an explorable lunar world.

Children find the equipment first. The questions come next.

A browser-based experience with no installation required
